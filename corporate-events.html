The Venue

The impression
your company deserves.

The George brings the same classical refinement to corporate gatherings that it brings to every event — because the impression your company makes matters. A distinguished venue signals that the work is serious and the organization is worthy of the moment.

8,400 square feet in the heart of downtown Birmingham. Steps from the Elyton Hotel. Across from The Pizitz Food Hall. Ideal for galas, client dinners, company milestones, product launches, award ceremonies, and VIP receptions — up to 500 attendees.

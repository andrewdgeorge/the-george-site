The Space

A setting that
stays with you.

The George is a classically refined event hall in downtown Birmingham, AL with every detail thoughtfully designed by Ragan George.

The George includes a dedicated bridal suite (The Parlor), a groom's room (The Lounge), a private outdoor space, a full catering prep kitchen, day-of security, and dedicated parking in the attached deck.

Across from The Pizitz Food Hall. Steps from the Elyton Hotel.

FAQ

Common questions,
honest answers.

Every rental includes exclusive use of the full 8,400-square-foot venue and a dedicated day-of coordinator. Tables and chairs are included. There are no room tiers. Every booking gets the whole building. Entry can be arranged beginning as early as 9am.
We do not provide catering. Clients select from our approved vendor list of Birmingham caterers. This gives you complete control over your menu, style, and budget without restrictions. Your caterer will have access to our caterer's prep kitchen the day of your event.
Bar service is provided in-house by The George's licensed staff. Clients may not bring outside alcohol. We offer four bar tiers: from Beer & Wine to The George Reserve, priced per hour per drinking guest. Use the bar estimator in our pricing section to build a full estimate, or see what each tier includes before you inquire.
Yes. The layout allows a full room flip between ceremony and reception. Our day-of coordinator manages the transition while you and your guests enjoy cocktail hour, or the courtyard, if you've added it. The bride's parlor is also available throughout the day for getting ready and pre-ceremony moments.
The George has dedicated parking spaces in the attached parking deck provided at no charge to guests. There are adjacent parking lots, which are monitored by day-of security. Street parking is also free on weekends and holidays, which covers most of our Saturday and Sunday events.
The George accommodates up to 350 guests for plated seated dinners and 500 guests for cocktail-style receptions. We do not exceed 500 guests to maintain the quality of the experience.
Yes. The George is a single-floor, ground-level venue with no stairs required for entry or circulation.
Submit an inquiry through our contact form with your preferred date and event details. We respond within one business day. If your date is available, we'll schedule a tour. No commitment required to visit.
Absolutely. We love working with planners and have relationships with many in Birmingham's planning community. If you're a planner interested in touring the space or joining our preferred vendor network, please reach out directly.
The courtyard is available as a paid add-on and can be used for outdoor ceremonies, cocktail hours, or open-air receptions. It's a reserved, dedicated space, ideal for couples who want their ceremony outside before moving indoors for the reception, or for clients who want a cocktail hour under the sky. Pricing and availability are included in your inquiry response.

The Story

Built by people
who love people.

Andrew and Ragan George are committed to building places of lasting beauty, purpose, and generational impact.

Their work is guided by a belief in returning to an era when buildings and spaces were designed not merely for use, but for legacy, crafted with elegance, intention, and permanence.

Their goal is to create enduring spaces that serve communities, honor tradition, and deliver thoughtful, long-term value for generations to come.
